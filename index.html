Help & Support

One phone. One imposter. Total chaos. Four fast party games for 2–12 players. No accounts, no setup, no internet.

The four games

Pressure Point

Everyone gets the same “most likely to…” prompt, except the Imposter, who only sees the category. Count down, then everyone points at once.

Scene Panic

Everyone secretly gets a role in the same scene. The Imposter only knows the theme and has to fake it. Act it out and spot the odd one out.

Suspects

Everyone shares a secret word, except the Imposter, who only gets the category. Discuss, vote out a suspect, and watch for hidden roles like the Jester, Mayor, and Rival.

Tap Out

A topic appears. Name something that fits and tap before the timer hits zero. Last player standing wins.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Pick a game, set your group size (2–12), and pass the phone around the room. Each game shows simple on-screen instructions, and there’s a “How to Play” section in the app for every mode.

Does PassPlay need an internet connection?

No. PassPlay works fully offline. The only time it uses the network is to process an optional in-app purchase through the App Store.

What are the premium packs?

The Freaky, Chaos, and Besties packs are optional one-time purchases that each add hundreds of extra prompts. Buying any single pack also unlocks Custom Topics, letting you add your own prompts to every game. The Classic pack is free and included.

I bought a pack but don’t see it / got a new phone.

Open Settings → Premium Status in the app and tap Restore Purchases. As long as you’re signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with, your packs will be restored. Purchases are one-time and tied to your Apple ID at no extra cost.

Can I turn off sound or vibration?

Yes. Open Settings → Preferences and toggle Sound Effects or Haptics.

How many players do I need?

Tap Out works with 2 or more. Pressure Point, Scene Panic, and Suspects need at least 3, so the Imposter has a group to hide in. Everything supports up to 12.

Do you collect my data?

No. PassPlay collects no personal information. Your preferences and custom topics stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

Still need help?
