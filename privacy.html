Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

PassPlay does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how PassPlay (“the app,” “we,” “us”), an app by Gomes Games, handles information. PassPlay is a pass-the-phone party game for two or more people that runs entirely on a single device. We built it to be played offline, with no accounts and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. PassPlay has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, or tracking software. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, device identifiers, or usage data.

Information stored on your device

To make the app work and remember your choices, PassPlay saves a small amount of data locally on your device only. This never leaves your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your selected group size and game preferences (such as sound and haptics toggles).
  • Which content packs are enabled.
  • Any custom topics or prompts you create.

This data is removed if you delete the app.

Purchases

PassPlay offers optional one-time in-app purchases (content packs). All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We never see or receive your payment information. Apple’s handling of purchase data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. “Restore Purchases” simply asks Apple which packs you already own.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we receive the email address and message you choose to send, and we use it only to reply to you. We do not add you to any list or share it with anyone.

Children

PassPlay is a general-audience party game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to request, correct, or delete from our side.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.

Contact
